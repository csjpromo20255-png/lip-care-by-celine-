Step-by-Step Guide

The 5-Step Lip Care Routine

Follow this simple daily and nightly routine for smooth, hydrated, glossy lips.

Step 1: Exfoliate

Massage a small amount of Strawberry Lip Sugar Scrub in gentle circles, then rinse or wipe clean.

Step 2: Line

Trace your natural lip contour with the Creamy Lip Liner for soft definition.

Step 3: Tint

Tap Berry Rose Lip Tint onto the center of your lips and blend outward.

Step 4: Gloss

Swipe Crystal Clear Lip Gloss over the top for dimensional shine.

Step 5: Hydrate & Restore

Apply Hydrating Lip Mask with the brush before sleep to lock in moisture overnight.
